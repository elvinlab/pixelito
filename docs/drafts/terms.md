## In short

Pixelito lets you play J2ME games in your browser. It is a beta: things can break and change. Be kind, respect other people's rights, and do not abuse the service.

## 1. Who can use it

You must be at least 16 years old. If you are not, please do not create an account.

## 2. Your own game files

- You can open a `.jar` file from your device. It runs **only in your browser** and is **never uploaded** to Pixelito.
- You are responsible for having the right to use the file. Pixelito does not host, sell or supply third-party games.
- The games in our catalogue are freely licensed. Each one shows its author, its licence and a link to its source.

## 3. Your account and saves

- An account is optional. Saves are kept in your browser first. Clearing your browser data can delete them, so use export when it is available.
- You may delete your account at any time. We then delete your account data (see the Privacy Notice).
- Do not share your account, and do not try to access other people's data.

## 4. Acceptable use

Do not: attack, overload or scrape the service; try to bypass its limits or security; cheat to gain platform achievements; use the service to break the law or someone's rights.

We may limit or close an account that does this.

## 5. The service as it is

Pixelito is provided "as is", free of charge, in beta. We do not promise that it is always available, that every game works, or that saves will never be lost. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses that come from using a free beta service.

## 6. Copyright complaints

If you think something on Pixelito infringes your rights, write to `[CONTACT]` with: what it is, where it is, why you hold the rights, and how to reach you. We will review it and remove content when the complaint is justified.

## 7. Software and licences

The emulator is open source (GPLv3) and its source is public. The Pixelito platform itself is not open source. Third-party components keep their own licences.

## 8. Changes

We may update these terms. For important changes we will tell you in the app before they apply. If you disagree, you can stop using Pixelito and delete your account.
